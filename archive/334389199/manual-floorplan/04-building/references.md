Searches for a public floor plan tied to this **specific apartment or stack**, or a verified series assignment for this address, did not turn up a verifiable drawing. A floor plan from another unit in this building would become useful only if its entrance, floor/stack, windows and utility core matched. Area similarity alone is insufficient. A future version of the app should search in this order: exact apartment plan → same vertical stack → same entrance and mirrored stack → building-specific two-room listings → likely series or section families. Each borrowed feature needs its own provenance and confidence.

**Effect on this example:** repeated brick-building layouts remain plausible. The СМ-3/СМ-6 family supplies only a weak area/period prior, with a lift-count mismatch to resolve; no address-specific wall, opening or dimension can yet be copied into either candidate. A matching section drawing would materially change this assessment.
