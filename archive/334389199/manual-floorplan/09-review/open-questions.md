## Unresolved

1. Does the bedroom's pictured door open directly into the kitchen, or onto a small tile-floored hall section? This single answer selects A or B.
2. Which side of the building contains this apartment? All drawings can be mirrored or rotated.
3. Are the kitchen and the two living-room windows on one continuous façade, a corner, or an inner recess?
4. What are the two separate room areas? The listing only gives their sum.
5. What is the actual order of bathroom, toilet and wardrobe doors along the hall?
6. Are the apartment walls rectangular? Perspective photos hide most corners behind furniture.

## Most valuable information obtainable without new photographs

- A public plan or historical listing of this exact apartment, or of the same vertical stack with matching windows and utility core.
- Existing cadastral/BTI drawing supplied by the seller or a previous advertisement.
- Exact model/width of a visible kitchen stove, bathtub or floor tile, if identifiable in seller-provided details. One measured segment helps scale a local wall, but still does not establish the whole apartment layout.

Until such evidence appears, the SVGs are best used as **candidate diagrams for comparison**, not as authoritative plans.
