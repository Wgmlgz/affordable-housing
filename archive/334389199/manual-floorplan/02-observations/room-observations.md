All three visible apartment windows are consistent with the listing's “courtyard” statement. Their locations on a common straight façade are **not** established; the kitchen window in [09](../../images/09.webp) looks toward a close brick face, whereas the two room windows show trees. The candidate drawings use a convenient courtyard-facing edge, without claiming north or an exact building outline.

The listing's “isolated rooms” is compatible with both a bedroom opening onto a kitchen and one opening onto a short corridor: neither requires passage through the living room.
